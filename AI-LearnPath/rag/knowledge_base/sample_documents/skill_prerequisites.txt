NEET UG 2026 Skill Prerequisites & Foundation Guide:

To maximize your score and concept retention in NEET preparation, NEET LearnPath recommends reviewing these prerequisite foundations:

1. NEET Physics Prerequisites:
Prerequisites: Basic Mathematics for Physics (Class 10-11 Algebra, Trigonometry, Vectors, Basic Differentiation & Integration d/dx and ∫dx).
Why: 70% of numerical problems in Mechanics, Electrostatics, and Modern Physics require fluency with trigonometry (sin, cos, tan values), vector addition/resolution, and rate of change.

2. NEET Physical & Inorganic Chemistry Prerequisites:
Prerequisites: Mole Concept basics, Periodic Table fundamentals (valency, groups/periods), Atomic Structure basics (Bohr model, electrons/protons/neutrons).
Why: Chemical equilibrium, thermodynamics, and periodic trends depend strictly on a clear grasp of molar mass calculations and electronic configuration.

3. NEET Organic Chemistry Prerequisites:
Prerequisites: IUPAC Nomenclature, hybridization (sp, sp2, sp3), electronic effects (inductive, resonance/mesomeric, hyperconjugation).
Why: Reaction mechanisms (SN1, SN2, electrophilic addition) become intuitive once electron displacement effects are mastered.

4. NEET Biology (Botany & Zoology) Prerequisites:
Prerequisites: Cell structure, cell division (mitosis/meiosis), basics of plant & animal kingdom classification.
Why: Genetics, plant physiology, human physiology, and ecology build directly on cellular mechanisms and taxonomic understanding.
