NEET UG 2026 Learning Path Guide:

When beginning NEET preparation on NEET LearnPath, follow this structured subject-wise study path aligned to the official NMC/NTA 2026 syllabus.

RECOMMENDED NEET STUDY ORDER:

Phase 1 — Foundation (Months 1-2):
1. Biology: Cell Structure & Function (Unit 3) — NCERT Class 11
2. Biology: Biological Classification & Diversity (Units 1-2) — NCERT Class 11
3. Chemistry: Some Basic Concepts (Unit 1) — Mole concept, stoichiometry
4. Chemistry: Atomic Structure (Unit 2) — Bohr model, quantum numbers
5. Physics: Physical World & Kinematics (Units 1-2) — Dimensions, equations of motion

Phase 2 — Core Syllabus (Months 3-5):
1. Physics: Laws of Motion, Work-Energy, Gravitation (Units 3-6)
2. Chemistry: Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics (Units 3-6)
3. Biology: Structural Organisation, Plant Physiology (Units 2-4) — NCERT Class 11
4. Biology: Human Physiology (Unit 5) — Digestion, Circulation, Breathing, Excretion

Phase 3 — Class 12 Syllabus (Months 5-8):
1. Physics: Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism (Units 11-14)
2. Chemistry: Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics, Surface Chemistry (Units 6-8)
3. Biology: Reproduction, Genetics & Evolution (Units 6-7) — NCERT Class 12
4. Chemistry: Organic Chemistry — GOC, Haloalkanes, Aldehydes, Biomolecules (Units 13-18)

Phase 4 — Revision & Mock Tests (Months 8-10):
1. Solve NEET PYQs 2019-2023 for all three subjects
2. Take full 180-question mock tests (NTA pattern: +4/-1)
3. Revise NEET traps and high-weightage chapters
4. Complete all NCERT exercises, exemplar problems, and in-text questions
5. Biology: Ecology & Biotechnology (Units 8-9) — NCERT Class 12

IMPORTANT NEET TIPS:
- NCERT is the Bible for NEET — read every line, diagram caption, and footnote
- Biology carries 360/720 marks — prioritise it heavily
- Chemistry Physical + Organic > Inorganic for scoring
- Physics: focus on Mechanics (25% weightage) and Electrodynamics (20%)
- Attempt 1 full mock test every week after Phase 2
