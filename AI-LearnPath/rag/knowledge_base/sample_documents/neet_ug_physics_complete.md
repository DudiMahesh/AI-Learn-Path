## Unit 1: Physics and Measurement

### 1.1 Units and Systems of Units
- Physical quantity = numerical value × unit.
- Fundamental quantities: length, mass, time, electric current, thermodynamic temperature, amount of substance, luminous intensity.
- Derived quantities: force, work, energy, power, pressure, frequency, charge.
- SI Base Units: metre (m), kilogram (kg), second (s), ampere (A), kelvin (K), mole (mol), candela (cd). Supplementary: radian (rad) and steradian (sr).

### 1.2 Least count of instruments
- Vernier calliper: LC = 1 MSD - 1 VSD = (value of 1 MSD) / (number of vernier divisions). Reading = MSR + (VSR × LC) - (Zero error).
- Screw gauge: LC = pitch / (number of circular scale divisions). Reading = PSR + (CSR × LC) ± zero correction.

### 1.3 Errors in measurement & Significant figures
- Absolute error: Δa = |a_measured - a_true|.
- Relative error = Δa_mean / a_mean. Percentage error = (Δa_mean / a_mean) × 100%.
- Error combination:
  - Z = A ± B → ΔZ = ΔA + ΔB
  - Z = (A^p B^q) / C^r → ΔZ / Z = p(ΔA/A) + q(ΔB/B) + r(ΔC/C).
- Significant figures rules: all non-zero digits are significant; zeros between non-zeros are significant; leading zeros are not significant; trailing zeros after decimal are significant.

### 1.4 Dimensions and dimensional analysis
- Principle of homogeneity: Every term on both sides of an equation must possess identical dimensions.
- Dimensions of key constants: Planck's constant h = [ML²T⁻¹], Gravitational constant G = [M⁻¹L³T⁻²], Viscosity η = [ML⁻¹T⁻¹], Torque = [ML²T⁻²].

## Unit 2: Kinematics

### 2.1 Motion in a straight line
- Distance (scalar) vs Displacement (vector).
- Instantaneous velocity v = dx/dt; acceleration a = dv/dt = d²x/dt².
- Equations of uniformly accelerated motion:
  1. v = u + at
  2. s = ut + ½at²
  3. v² = u² + 2as
  4. Distance in nth second: s_n = u + (a/2)(2n - 1)
- Graphs: Slope of x-t is velocity; slope of v-t is acceleration; area under v-t is displacement; area under a-t is change in velocity.

### 2.2 Vectors & Projectile motion
- Dot product: A · B = AB cos θ. Cross product: A × B = AB sin θ n̂.
- Projectile motion (launched with speed u at angle θ to horizontal):
  - Time of flight: T = 2u sin θ / g
  - Maximum height: H = u² sin²θ / 2g
  - Horizontal range: R = u² sin 2θ / g
  - Trajectory equation: y = x tan θ - g x² / (2 u² cos²θ)
  - Relation: R = 4H / tan θ; Maximum range at θ = 45° is R_max = u² / g.

### 2.3 Uniform circular motion
- Angular velocity ω = dθ/dt = 2π/T = 2πf. Linear speed v = ω r.
- Centripetal acceleration a_c = v² / r = ω² r, directed towards the centre.

## Unit 3: Laws of Motion

### 3.1 Newton's laws & Momentum
- First Law: Inertia (rest, motion, direction). Defines inertial frames.
- Second Law: F = dp/dt = m a. Impulse J = F Δt = Δp (area under F-t graph).
- Third Law: To every action there is an equal and opposite reaction acting on different bodies.
- Conservation of linear momentum: Recoil of gun V = -(m_b v_b) / M. Rocket thrust F = -u (dm/dt).

### 3.2 Friction & Circular dynamics
- Static friction: f_s ≤ μ_s N (self-adjusting). Limiting friction f_max = μ_s N.
- Kinetic friction: f_k = μ_k N. Angle of repose α: tan α = μ_s.
- Rough incline acceleration: a = g(sin θ - μ cos θ).
- Circular motion on level road: v_max = √(μ r g).
- Banked road without friction: tan θ = v² / (r g) → v = √(r g tan θ).
- Banked road with friction: v_max = √[r g (μ + tan θ) / (1 - μ tan θ)].

## Unit 4: Work, Energy and Power

### 4.1 Work and Kinetic energy
- Work W = F · s = F s cos θ. Variable force W = ∫ F dx (area under F-x curve).
- Kinetic energy K = ½ m v² = p² / 2m.
- Work-energy theorem: W_net = ΔK = ½ m v_f² - ½ m v_i².
- Power P = dW/dt = F · v = F v cos θ.

### 4.2 Conservative forces & Vertical circular motion
- Conservative force: F = -dU/dx; work in a closed loop is zero.
- Motion in vertical circle (string length r):
  - v_top ≥ √(g r)
  - v_bottom ≥ √(5 g r)
  - Tension difference: T_bottom - T_top = 6 m g.
- Collisions: Linear momentum conserved in all collisions.
  - Elastic collision (e = 1): KE conserved. Equal masses exchange velocities.
  - Inelastic collision (0 < e < 1): KE lost.
  - Perfectly inelastic (e = 0): Bodies stick together.

## Unit 5: Rotational Motion

### 5.1 Center of mass & Torque
- Center of mass: r_cm = Σ m_i r_i / Σ m_i. Motion: M a_cm = F_ext.
- Torque τ = r × F = I α = dL/dt.
- Angular momentum L = r × p = I ω. Conserved when net external torque is zero.

### 5.2 Moment of inertia & Rolling motion
- Moments of inertia:
  - Ring (central axis): MR²; (diameter): ½MR²
  - Disc (central axis): ½MR²; (diameter): ¼MR²
  - Solid cylinder: ½MR²; Hollow cylinder: MR²
  - Solid sphere (diameter): (2/5)MR²; Hollow sphere: (2/3)MR²
  - Thin rod (center): ML²/12; (end): ML²/3
- Parallel axes theorem: I = I_cm + M d². Perpendicular axes theorem: I_z = I_x + I_y (planar bodies only).
- Pure rolling: v_cm = ω R. Total KE = ½ M v² (1 + K²/R²). Acceleration down incline a = g sin θ / (1 + K²/R²).

## Unit 6: Gravitation

### 6.1 Universal gravitation & Variation of g
- F = G m₁ m₂ / r² (G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² kg⁻²).
- Surface gravity: g = G M / R² ≈ 9.8 m s⁻².
- Variation with height: g_h = g (1 - 2h/R) for h << R.
- Variation with depth: g_d = g (1 - d/R). Zero at Earth center.
- Variation with latitude: g' = g - ω² R cos² λ.

### 6.2 Kepler's laws, Escape velocity & Satellites
- Kepler's laws: (1) Elliptical orbits; (2) Areal velocity dA/dt = L / 2m = constant; (3) T² ∝ a³.
- Escape velocity: v_e = √(2 G M / R) = √(2 g R) ≈ 11.2 km/s.
- Satellite orbital speed: v_o = √(G M / r) ≈ √(g R) ≈ 7.9 km/s. Relation: v_e = √2 v_o.
- Total energy of satellite = -G M m / 2r = -KE = ½PE.

## Unit 7: Properties of Solids and Liquids

### 7.1 Elasticity & Fluid pressure
- Hooke's law: Stress ∝ Strain. Young's modulus Y = (F/A)/(Δl/l). Energy density u = ½ Y (strain)².
- Fluid pressure P = h ρ g. Pascal's law: F₂/A₂ = F₁/A₁ (hydraulic lift).

### 7.2 Viscosity, Stokes law & Bernoulli's principle
- Viscous force F = -η A (dv/dx). Stokes' law: F = 6 π η r v.
- Terminal velocity: v_t = 2 r² (ρ - σ) g / (9 η).
- Equation of continuity: A₁ v₁ = A₂ v₂.
- Bernoulli's theorem: P + ½ ρ v² + ρ g h = constant.
- Torricelli's speed of efflux: v = √(2gh).

### 7.3 Surface tension & Thermal expansion
- Surface tension S = F/l. Work to blow soap bubble: W = 8π R² S.
- Excess pressure: Drop ΔP = 2S/R; Bubble ΔP = 4S/R.
- Capillary rise: h = 2 S cos θ / (r ρ g).
- Thermal expansion: Δl = l α ΔT; ΔA = A (2α) ΔT; ΔV = V (3α) ΔT.
- Heat transfer: Conduction Q/t = K A (T₁ - T₂) / l; Stefan-Boltzmann E = σ T⁴; Wien's displacement λ_m T = constant.

## Unit 8: Thermodynamics
- First Law: Q = ΔU + W (W = P ΔV). For ideal gas: ΔU = n Cv ΔT.
- Specific heats: Cp - Cv = R; γ = Cp / Cv (monatomic 5/3, diatomic 7/5, polyatomic 4/3).
- Processes:
  - Isothermal: PV = const, ΔU = 0, W = nRT ln(V₂/V₁).
  - Adiabatic: PV^γ = const, Q = 0, W = (P₁V₁ - P₂V₂) / (γ - 1).
- Carnot engine efficiency: η = 1 - T₂ / T₁ (T in Kelvin). Refrigerator β = T₂ / (T₁ - T₂).

---

## Unit 9: Kinetic Theory of Gases
- Ideal gas equation: P V = n R T = N k_B T (k_B = 1.38 × 10⁻²³ J K⁻¹).
- Pressure: P = ⅓ ρ v_rms² = ⅔ (N/V)(½ m v_rms²).
- Speeds: v_rms = √(3RT/M); v_avg = √(8RT/πM); v_mp = √(2RT/M).
- Degrees of freedom: Monatomic f = 3, Diatomic f = 5. Law of equipartition: energy per degree of freedom = ½ k_B T.
- Mean free path: λ = 1 / (√2 π d² n).

## Unit 10: Oscillations and Waves
- SHM: a = -ω² x. Displacement x = A sin(ωt + φ). Velocity v = ω √(A² - x²).
- Energy: K = ½ m ω² (A² - x²); U = ½ m ω² x²; Total E = ½ m ω² A² = ½ k A².
- Simple pendulum: T = 2π √(l / g). Spring-mass: T = 2π √(m / k).
- Wave speed: v = f λ = ω / k. Sound speed v = √(γ P / ρ).
- Standing waves:
  - Open pipe: f₁ = v / 2L; all harmonics (1, 2, 3...).
  - Closed pipe: f₁ = v / 4L; odd harmonics only (1, 3, 5...).
- Beats: frequency = |f₁ - f₂|.

---

## Unit 11: Electrostatics
- Coulomb's law: F = k q₁ q₂ / r² (k = 9 × 10⁹ N m² C⁻²).
- Electric field: Point charge E = k q / r². Dipole moment p = q(2a).
  - Axial: E_axial = 2kp / r³. Equatorial: E_eq = kp / r³. Torque τ = p × E.
- Gauss's law: Φ = ∮ E · dA = q_enc / ε₀. Wire: E = 2kλ / r; Sheet: E = σ / 2ε₀; Shell: inside 0, outside kQ / r².
- Potential V = kq / r; E = -dV/dr. Potential energy U = k q₁ q₂ / r.
- Capacitance C = Q / V. Parallel plate: C = K ε₀ A / d. Energy U = ½ C V² = Q² / 2C.

---

## Unit 12: Current Electricity
- Current I = n e A v_d. Drift velocity v_d = e E τ / m.
- Ohm's law: V = I R. Resistance R = ρ l / A. Stretching wire by factor n gives R' = n² R.
- Combinations: Series R = Σ R_i; Parallel 1/R = Σ 1/R_i.
- Cells: Terminal voltage V = ε - I r (discharging).
- Kirchhoff's laws: Junction rule (charge conservation), Loop rule (energy conservation).
- Metre bridge: R / S = l / (100 - l) → S = R (100 - l) / l.

---

## Unit 13: Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism
- Biot-Savart law: dB = (μ₀/4π) I dl sin θ / r². Center of loop: B = μ₀ N I / 2R. Straight wire: B = μ₀ I / 2π r.
- Ampere's circuital law: ∮ B · dl = μ₀ I_enc. Solenoid B = μ₀ n I.
- Lorentz force: F = q (E + v × B). Radius of circular path r = m v / q B. Cyclotron frequency f = q B / 2π m.
- Force on current: F = I (l × B). Parallel currents attract: F/l = μ₀ I₁ I₂ / 2π d.
- Moving coil galvanometer: τ = N I A B = k θ. Shunt S = I_g G / (I - I_g). Series R = V / I_g - G.
- Magnetic materials: Diamagnetic (χ < 0), Paramagnetic (χ > 0, ∝ 1/T), Ferromagnetic (large χ, Curie temp).

---

## Unit 14: Electromagnetic Induction and Alternating Currents
- Faraday's law: ε = -N dΦ/dt. Lenz's law indicates direction (conservation of energy). Motional emf ε = B l v.
- Inductance: ε = -L dI/dt; Energy U = ½ L I². Solenoid L = μ₀ n² A l.
- Alternating current: i_rms = i₀ / √2 ≈ 0.707 i₀.
- Series LCR: Impedance Z = √[R² + (X_L - X_C)²]. Resonance at ω₀ = 1 / √(LC), Z = R, power factor cos φ = 1.
- Transformer: V_s / V_p = N_s / N_p = I_p / I_s.

---

## Unit 15: Electromagnetic Waves
- Displacement current: I_d = ε₀ dΦ_E / dt.
- Maxwell's prediction: E and B oscillate mutually perpendicular and perpendicular to propagation direction.
- c = 1 / √(μ₀ ε₀) = 3 × 10⁸ m/s; E₀ / B₀ = c.
- Spectrum order: Radio < Microwave < Infrared < Visible (VIBGYOR) < Ultraviolet < X-rays < Gamma rays.

---

## Unit 16: Optics
- Reflection: Mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f; magnification m = -v/u.
- Refraction: Snell's law n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r. Apparent depth = real depth / n.
- Total internal reflection (TIR): sin θ_c = 1/n. Optical fibres, diamonds.
- Thin lens: 1/v - 1/u = 1/f; magnification m = v/u. Lens maker's formula 1/f = (n - 1)(1/R₁ - 1/R₂). Power P = 1/f (dioptres).
- Prism: n = sin[(A + δ_m)/2] / sin(A/2). Small angle δ = (n - 1) A.
- Optical instruments: Compound microscope M = -(v₀/u₀)(1 + D/f_e); Telescope M = -f₀ / f_e.
- Wave optics: YDSE fringe width β = λ D / d. Single slit central maximum width = 2 λ D / a. Brewster's law tan i_B = n.

---

## Unit 17: Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation
- Photoelectric effect: hν = W₀ + K_max = hν₀ + e V₀. K_max depends on frequency; photocurrent depends on intensity.
- de Broglie wavelength: λ = h / p = h / mv = h / √(2mK) = h / √(2mqV).
- For electron: λ = 12.27 / √V Å.

---

## Unit 18: Atoms and Nuclei
- Rutherford scattering: Nucleus is dense positive core. Distance of closest approach r₀ = 2Ze² / (4πε₀ K).
- Bohr hydrogen model: r_n = 0.529 n²/Z Å; E_n = -13.6 Z²/n² eV.
- Hydrogen spectrum: 1/λ = R Z² (1/n₁² - 1/n₂²). Lyman (UV), Balmer (Visible), Paschen (IR).
- Nuclear radius R = R₀ A^(1/3). Density is constant (~2.3 × 10¹⁷ kg/m³).
- Mass defect Δm; Binding energy B = Δm × 931.5 MeV. Fe-56 has peak stability (8.8 MeV/nucleon). Fission splits heavy nuclei; fusion combines light nuclei.

---

## Unit 19: Electronic Devices
- Semiconductors: Intrinsic n_e = n_h = n_i. Extrinsic n-type (pentavalent) and p-type (trivalent).
- p-n junction: Forward bias narrows depletion barrier; Reverse bias widens barrier.
- Diode as rectifier: Half-wave (eff ≈ 40.6%), Full-wave (eff ≈ 81.2%, ripple 2f).
- Special diodes: Zener (voltage regulator in reverse breakdown), LED (forward bias emits photons), Photodiode (reverse bias light detector), Solar cell (photovoltaic).
- Logic gates: AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR. Universal gates: NAND, NOR.

## Unit 20: Experimental Skills
- Vernier calipers and screw gauge least count.
- Simple pendulum energy dissipation (amplitude² vs time exponential decay).
- Metre scale mass determination via moments: M₁ l₁ = M₂ l₂.
- Searle's method for Young's modulus: Y = (M g l) / (π r² Δl).
- Surface tension by capillary rise: S = r h ρ g / 2.
- Viscosity by terminal velocity: η = 2 r² (ρ - σ) g / 9 v_t.
- Speed of sound by resonance tube: v = 2 f (l₂ - l₁).
- Specific heat by method of mixtures.
- Metre bridge resistance and resistivity: S = R(100 - l)/l.
- Galvanometer half-deflection: G = R S / (R - S).
- Focal length of lenses and mirrors by parallax method.
- Diode V-I characteristics and identification of components using multimeter.
